HackBox uses a locally bundled snapshot of selected World Bank World Development Indicators
to make its hidden, fictional gameplay balance more geographically varied.

Indicators used:
- SP.POP.TOTL — Population, total
- NY.GDP.PCAP.CD — GDP per capita (current US$)
- IT.NET.USER.ZS — Individuals using the Internet (% of population)

Source: World Bank Indicators API v2
https://api.worldbank.org/v2/
Retrieved: 2026-08-29 UTC

The resulting values are internal game coefficients only. They are not presented as
country cybersecurity ratings or factual assessments of a country’s institutions.
